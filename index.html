Our story

Fire, sea and good vibes

PAL'MAR was born from a simple idea: to celebrate the bay's fresh seafood with the honesty of a wood fire. Every dish is cooked over wood embers, letting the smoke speak for itself.

We are a small restaurant with contemporary design, made for sharing. Our dishes are meant for the center of the table, paired with fresh cocktails and good music.

At PAL'MAR

Seafood, music and slow evenings

PAL'MAR is built around the rhythm of the coast: fresh catches, open-fire cooking, and a relaxed atmosphere that turns dinner into a shared experience. From the first cocktail to the last bite, every detail is designed to feel generous, warm and memorable.

We serve a menu inspired by the sea and the region, with dishes meant for gathering, conversation and lingering a little longer than planned. Whether you come for a sunset drink, a table for friends or a special dinner, the experience is always rooted in the fire, the ocean and its flavors.

The experience
